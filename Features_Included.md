### These are all the gameplay and project features that I added to the game project





Visual Effects

* VHS PostProcessing Effect - This includes worn out tape effects, chromatic aberration, a "band" of tape that moves up the screen and random noise. There is also a feature that converts the color grading to YIQ and back to RGB, but it is currently not applied.





Gameplay Features

* Movement - the ability to walk around.
* Look - the ability to look around.
* Sprint - the ability to sprint temporarily.
* Flashlight - the ability to turn a flashlight on and off





Sound Effects

* Flashlight Sounds - Flashlight has a sound play when you turn it on and off.







Misc

* Custom Physical Materials were added to project settings
